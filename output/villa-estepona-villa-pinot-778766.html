About the Property

Available for long-term rental from 1st September.

Discover the epitome of Scandinavian elegance in Villa Pinot, a beautifully designed 4-bedroom villa located in the prestigious La Resina area, perfectly positioned between Marbella and Estepona.

This exceptional home combines modern sophistication with Mediterranean living, offering spacious interiors, high-quality finishes and a warm, contemporary design throughout. The chef-designed kitchen is the heart of the home, seamlessly connecting to the open-plan living and dining area, complete with a fireplace that creates a welcoming atmosphere all year round.

Large sliding doors open onto the covered outdoor dining terrace with a barbecue area, overlooking the private swimming pool and landscaped gardens. The generous outdoor spaces provide the perfect setting for relaxing, entertaining or enjoying the Costa del Sol lifestyle.

The villa offers four spacious bedrooms and three bathrooms. Upstairs, the master suite features an en-suite bathroom and a private balcony seating area. Two additional bedrooms share a family bathroom, while the rooftop terrace offers panoramic views across the golf course towards Gibraltar and the African coastline. On the lower level, a fourth bedroom with its own bathroom provides additional privacy for guests or family members.

The property also benefits from a private garage and a fully equipped home gym, making it ideal for comfortable year-round living.

Ideally situated just minutes from golf courses, beaches, international schools, supermarkets, restaurants and the centres of Marbella, Puerto Banús and Estepona, Villa Pinot offers the perfect balance of privacy, comfort and convenience for those looking to enjoy life on the Costa del Sol.
