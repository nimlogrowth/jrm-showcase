About the Property

Welcome to Penthouse Oceana Views, a beautifully designed newly built 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom penthouse perfectly positioned between Marbella and Estepona. Combining contemporary style, breathtaking sea views, and an exceptional location, this penthouse offers the ideal Costa del Sol escape.

The apartment features a fresh and modern interior with bright open living spaces, elegant finishes, and a stylish contemporary design throughout. Large windows fill the home with natural light while seamlessly connecting the indoor and outdoor areas.

One of the highlights of the property is the spacious private roof terrace, the perfect place to relax, dine outdoors, or enjoy unforgettable west-facing sunsets overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. Whether you are sunbathing during the day or enjoying evening drinks with panoramic views, the outdoor spaces truly make this penthouse special.

Ideally located just a few minutes’ drive from the beach and the world-famous Puerto Banús, the property also offers easy access to both Marbella and Estepona town, allowing guests to enjoy the very best of the Costa del Sol. From golf courses and beach clubs to restaurants, shopping, and charming old towns, everything is within easy reach while still enjoying a peaceful and relaxing setting.

Penthouse Oceana Views is the perfect choice for guests seeking modern luxury, stunning views, and a prime location on the Costa del Sol.

Please note: This property welcomes families and couples only. We do not accept groups under 30 or hen/stag parties. If you fall outside this category but would still like to book, please send us an enquiry with details about your group so we can consider your request.
