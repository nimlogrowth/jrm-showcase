About the Property

Indulge in the epitome of luxury at Villa Sophia, nestled in the esteemed Paraiso Alto neighborhood of Marbella. Recently completed in May 2022, this remarkable villa seamlessly combines sophistication and comfort, catering to the needs of discerning travelers seeking excellence. Whether you seek serene relaxation or opulent entertainment, Villa Sophia stands ready to fulfill your vacation aspirations.

Occupying a prime position atop a private 2,800m2 plot, this 1,400m2 villa offers accommodation for up to 12 guests (+3) across six luxurious bedrooms, each featuring walk-in wardrobes and panoramic views spanning from the hinterland to the sea.

Upon arrival, you're greeted by an expansive entrance that unveils an exclusive hall spanning multiple levels with ceilings reaching up to 6 meters high. Dominating the space is "Sophia Loren," a 12m2 wall mural paying homage to the iconic Italian actress after whom the villa is named.

The heart of Villa Sophia is undeniably its spacious kitchen, equipped with Wolf and Subzero appliances, complemented by two dining areas—one adjacent to the fireplace and another boasting sea views and vistas of the La Concha mountains. Adjoining the kitchen is a sprawling living area, providing a total of 300m2 of open space for relaxation and entertainment.

The crown jewel of the villa is the expansive 130m2 Master bedroom, complete with an outdoor shower and a private terrace offering breathtaking views.

Outside, a generously sized pool beckons with 20m of swimming lanes, accompanied by a separate hot tub boasting panoramic vistas. Indulge in the private spa facilities, featuring a sauna, a Moroccan-inspired steam bath, an ice water basin, and a spacious gym overlooking the sea and the La Concha mountains.

For entertainment, Villa Sophia offers a pool table, a TV room, a table tennis table, and a chic chill-out area, ensuring every moment is filled with enjoyment.

Whether admiring the sea views from every corner of the villa, embarking on hiking adventures in the nearby La Concha Mountain, or perfecting your golf swing at one of the renowned nearby golf clubs, Villa Sophia promises an unforgettable escape.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only, no groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties. Should you fall outside of this category but wish to book nonetheless, please send us an enquiry detailing a bit more about yourself and the group that you will be travelling with so we can request this for you.
