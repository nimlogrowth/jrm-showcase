About the Property

Duplex Penthouse with Jacuzzi, BBQ & Panoramic Sea Views in La Resina

Welcome to this stylish duplex penthouse in the sought-after area of La Resina, perfectly positioned between Marbella and Estepona. Combining contemporary Scandinavian-inspired interiors with expansive outdoor living spaces, this beautiful apartment offers the ideal setting for a relaxing Costa del Sol getaway.

Located within a secure gated community with a large communal swimming pool, the property enjoys a peaceful setting while remaining just a short drive from the beaches, golf courses, restaurants, beach clubs, and attractions of both Marbella and Estepona.

Designed with comfort and elegance in mind, the penthouse features bright open-plan living spaces, modern furnishings, and floor-to-ceiling windows that fill the apartment with natural light while showcasing the stunning views beyond. The fully equipped kitchen provides everything needed for a comfortable stay, while the inviting living and dining areas seamlessly connect to the outdoor terraces, creating the effortless indoor-outdoor lifestyle that Marbella is famous for.

The apartment offers two spacious bedrooms and two modern bathrooms, making it ideal for couples, families, golfers, and friends looking to enjoy the best of the Costa del Sol. Air conditioning, high-speed WiFi, private parking, and all modern conveniences ensure a comfortable and carefree stay.

One of the standout features of this penthouse is its exceptional outdoor space spread across multiple terraces. On the main floor, a charming breakfast terrace provides the perfect spot to start the day with a coffee while enjoying the open views across the surrounding landscape and towards the Mediterranean Sea.

The true highlight awaits upstairs on the spectacular rooftop terrace. This private outdoor retreat has been designed for relaxation, entertaining, and making the most of Marbella's year-round sunshine. Here you can unwind in your private Jacuzzi, stretch out on the sun loungers, enjoy drinks in the lounge area, prepare meals on the BBQ, or gather around the outdoor dining table for long lunches and memorable evenings under the stars. From every corner of the terrace, you'll enjoy beautiful open views over the surrounding countryside, coastline, and the Mediterranean Sea beyond.

La Resina is a peaceful residential area ideally situated between Marbella and Estepona, offering the perfect balance between tranquility and convenience. Within a short drive you can reach beautiful sandy beaches, renowned golf courses, Estepona's charming old town, Puerto Banús, Marbella's historic centre, beach clubs, restaurants, shopping centres, and supermarkets.

Whether you're soaking in the Jacuzzi as the sun sets over the coast, enjoying a barbecue with friends, or simply relaxing with a glass of wine while taking in the views, this elegant penthouse offers everything needed for an unforgettable holiday on the Costa del Sol.
