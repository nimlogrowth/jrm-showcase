About the Property

Welcome to your private luxury escape in the hills of La Quinta, one of the most exclusive residential areas near Marbella. This stunning contemporary villa is designed for unforgettable holidays, combining modern architecture, complete privacy, and effortless indoor-outdoor living just minutes from the coast.

As you enter the villa, you are greeted by a bright and spacious open-plan living area where floor-to-ceiling glass doors flood the space with natural light and seamlessly connect the interior with expansive terraces. The design is elegant yet relaxed, creating the perfect atmosphere for both entertaining and unwinding. The fully equipped designer kitchen, stylish lounge, and refined dining area all open onto the outdoor spaces, where you can enjoy long sunny days and warm evenings under the Mediterranean sky.

The villa features beautifully designed bedrooms, each offering comfort, privacy, and direct access to terraces or views of the surrounding hills. Outside, you’ll find a private pool, multiple chill-out areas, and inviting lounge spaces that make it easy to spend entire days outdoors, whether you’re sunbathing, dining al fresco, or enjoying a quiet morning coffee.

What truly sets this villa apart is the experience it offers. A private cinema room with a bar and entertainment area creates the perfect setting for evenings in, while the outdoor living spaces invite you to relax and socialize in total privacy. Every detail has been carefully considered to ensure a luxurious yet effortless stay.

Located in a secure urbanisation, the villa offers a sense of seclusion while still being just a short drive from Puerto Banús, beaches, restaurants, and some of the best golf courses on the Costa del Sol. It is the ideal base for guests who want both tranquility and easy access to the vibrant lifestyle of Marbella.

Whether you are planning a relaxing getaway, a family holiday, or a special trip with friends, this villa offers a unique blend of luxury, comfort, and location, creating a space where unforgettable memories are made.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only, no groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties. Should you fall outside of this category but wish to book nonetheless, please send us an enquiry detailing a bit more about yourself and the group that you will be travelling with so we can request this for you.
