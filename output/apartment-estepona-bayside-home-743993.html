About the Property

Welcome to Bayside Home, a stylish and modern 3-bedroom apartment located in the sought-after Bayside Homes urbanisation near Estepona. Combining contemporary design, comfortable outdoor living, and excellent community facilities, this apartment is the perfect base for enjoying the Costa del Sol lifestyle.

The apartment features bright open-plan living spaces with a modern fully equipped kitchen that seamlessly connects to the dining and lounge areas, creating a spacious and social atmosphere ideal for families, couples, or groups of friends.

Step outside onto the private terrace, where you can relax in the outdoor lounge and dining area, enjoy meals under the sun, or make the most of warm evenings with the BBQ. The terrace offers the perfect setting for outdoor living and relaxing Mediterranean days.

Guests also have access to excellent communal facilities including a large outdoor swimming pool and a fully equipped gym, allowing you to combine relaxation and wellness throughout your stay.

Located just minutes from Estepona’s beaches, restaurants, golf courses, and charming town centre, Bayside Home offers the ideal balance between peaceful residential living and easy access to everything the Costa del Sol has to offer.

Whether you’re planning a relaxing beach escape, a family holiday, or a longer stay in the sun, Bayside Home provides the perfect modern retreat near Estepona.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only. No groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties are allowed. Should you fall outside of this category but still wish to book, please send us an enquiry with more details about yourself and your group.
