About the Property

Available from 1 October, this beautifully presented three-bedroom apartment is located in the sought-after Residencial Senda, one of Estepona's newest residential developments. Combining contemporary architecture with warm Mediterranean interiors, it offers the perfect home for those looking to enjoy modern living in one of the Costa del Sol's most desirable locations.

Designed with comfort and practicality in mind, the apartment features a bright open-plan living and dining area with a fully equipped modern kitchen, creating a welcoming space for everyday living. Large sliding doors lead onto a spacious covered terrace overlooking the athletics stadium and surrounding green areas, providing the perfect place to relax throughout the year.

The property offers three stylish bedrooms and two modern bathrooms with spacious walk-in showers. The master bedroom features an extra-long 210 cm bed, while the two guest bedrooms provide comfortable accommodation for family members or visitors.

For added convenience, the apartment includes a private underground parking space and a private storage room, ideal for bicycles, golf equipment or additional storage.

Residencial Senda enjoys one of Estepona's most convenient locations, within walking distance of the charming Old Town, beautiful beaches, the marina, supermarkets, cafés and restaurants. Whether you're relocating, working remotely or simply looking to enjoy the Costa del Sol lifestyle, this apartment offers an excellent combination of comfort, quality and location.
