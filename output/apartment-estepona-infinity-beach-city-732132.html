About the Property

Experience the best of Estepona from this beautifully designed, newly built 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment located right in the heart of town. Perfectly positioned just minutes from the beach, restaurants, cafés, shops, and all local amenities, this is the ideal base for enjoying the vibrant Costa del Sol lifestyle.

The apartment combines modern comfort with elegant design, featuring a bright open-plan living area, a fully equipped contemporary kitchen, and stylish finishes throughout. Large windows and thoughtful interiors create a welcoming and relaxing atmosphere, perfect for both short and extended stays.

Guests can enjoy access to exceptional communal facilities, including a stunning rooftop swimming pool with panoramic sea views, gym, sauna, and a modern co-working space—ideal for remote workers or anyone looking to combine leisure with productivity.

Whether you’re looking for a beach holiday, a city escape, or a stylish place to work remotely under the Spanish sun, this apartment offers everything you need in one of Estepona’s most sought-after locations.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only, no groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties. Should you fall outside of this category but wish to book nonetheless, please send us an enquiry detailing a bit more about yourself and the group that you will be travelling with so we can request this for you.
