About the Property

Available for long-term rental from 1st September.

Located in the heart of Marbella's prestigious Golden Mile, this exceptional south-facing villa offers an outstanding opportunity to enjoy one of the Costa del Sol's most sought-after residential areas. Combining elegant architecture, generous living spaces and a prime location, it is perfectly suited for comfortable year-round living.

Built with exceptional attention to detail, the villa features impressive high ceilings, premium finishes and a timeless design that creates a warm yet sophisticated atmosphere throughout. Upon entering, you are welcomed by a grand entrance hall overlooking the living room and staircase, immediately showcasing the character of the property.

The ground floor offers a spacious bedroom with a walk-in wardrobe and an en-suite bathroom featuring both a bathtub and a walk-in shower. The room enjoys pleasant views over the private garden and swimming pool. The stylish living room is divided into comfortable lounge and dining areas and opens directly onto the outdoor terrace, while the fully equipped kitchen provides both functionality and elegant design.

Outside, the villa boasts a private swimming pool surrounded by sun terraces, landscaped gardens and plenty of space for outdoor dining and relaxation, offering complete privacy throughout the year.

On the first floor are two additional spacious bedrooms, each with wooden flooring and its own en-suite bathroom. The fourth bedroom is located on the lower level and also benefits from an en-suite bathroom, making it ideal for guests, staff, older children or anyone looking for additional privacy.

The property is fully air-conditioned and offers high-speed Wi-Fi, secure gated parking for one vehicle, plus an additional parking space in the private garage.

Ideally positioned just minutes from Marbella town centre, Puerto Banús, international schools, golf courses, restaurants, shopping and the beach, this villa offers the perfect balance of privacy, luxury and convenience for those seeking an exceptional long-term home on the Costa del Sol.
