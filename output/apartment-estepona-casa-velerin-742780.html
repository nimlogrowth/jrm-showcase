About the Property

Welcome to Casa Velerin, a beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom beachfront apartment located in the sought-after Velerin area, perfectly positioned between Marbella and Estepona. Offering direct beach access, breathtaking sea views, and stylish contemporary interiors, this apartment is the ideal setting for a relaxing Costa del Sol escape.

Designed with comfort and elegance in mind, the apartment features bright open living spaces, modern finishes, and a fresh contemporary style throughout. Large windows and outdoor spaces allow you to fully enjoy the incredible Mediterranean views and the calming atmosphere of beachfront living.

One of the highlights of Casa Velerin is its unbeatable frontline beach location. Right in front of the community, a beautiful seaside boulevard offers direct access for morning walks, beach days, cycling, or sunset strolls along the coast.

Each of the three bedrooms and bathrooms has been thoughtfully designed to create a comfortable and private retreat, making the apartment perfect for families, couples, or groups travelling together.

Located in the peaceful Velerin region, guests can enjoy a more relaxed beachfront setting while still being only a short drive from both Estepona and Marbella, with their restaurants, beach clubs, golf courses, shopping, and vibrant atmosphere all within easy reach.

Casa Velerin is the perfect choice for guests looking for modern beachfront living, spectacular sea views, and direct access to the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only, no groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties. Should you fall outside of this category but wish to book nonetheless, please send us an enquiry detailing a bit more about yourself and the group that you will be travelling with so we can request this for you.
