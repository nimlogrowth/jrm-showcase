About the Property

Welcome to Villa Velora, a stunning 5-bedroom frontline golf villa designed for guests seeking modern luxury, privacy, and unforgettable views in one of Marbella’s most exclusive settings. Perfectly positioned within a secure gated community, this exceptional villa combines elegant contemporary architecture with breathtaking golf and sunset views to create a truly unique Costa del Sol experience.

Flooded with natural light, the villa features spacious open-plan living areas, sleek modern finishes, and beautifully designed interiors throughout. Large floor-to-ceiling windows seamlessly connect the indoor and outdoor spaces while showcasing magnificent southwest-facing views over the golf course, creating incredible sunsets every evening.

Designed for both relaxation and entertaining, Villa Velora offers multiple stylish terraces and outdoor lounge areas where guests can unwind in complete privacy. The outdoor BBQ station makes alfresco dining effortless, whether enjoying long summer lunches or sunset dinners overlooking the greens.

The villa also features a private sauna and elevator, adding both comfort and convenience to your stay. Each bedroom has been thoughtfully designed with a calm and luxurious atmosphere, while the elegant bathrooms and high-end finishes elevate the experience throughout the home.

Located frontline golf in a prestigious gated community, Villa Velora offers a peaceful and exclusive environment while remaining close to Marbella’s beaches, restaurants, golf courses, and Puerto Banús.

Villa Velora is the perfect choice for guests looking for a sophisticated modern villa with spectacular views, luxury amenities, and one of the best sunset settings on the Costa del Sol.

Please note that we welcome families and couples only, no groups under 30, and no hen or stag parties. Should you fall outside of this category but wish to book nonetheless, please send us an enquiry detailing a bit more about yourself and the group that you will be travelling with so we can request this for you.
